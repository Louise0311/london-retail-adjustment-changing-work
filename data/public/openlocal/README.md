# OpenLocal public analysis tables

These files are public, OpenLocal-only analysis tables prepared from the openly licensed commercial-property source:

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `openlocal_msoa_annual_retail_indicators_2019_2025.csv` | Study-scope annual retail unit count, floorspace, rateable value and occupation-based vacancy indicators for 2019 and 2023-2025 |
| `openlocal_msoa_retail_yearly_change_from_2019.csv` | Annual 2023-2025 retail changes relative to the 2019 baseline |
| `openlocal_msoa_retail_2023_2025_mean_change_from_2019.csv` | Mean post-pandemic retail change used in the residential-origin analysis |
| `openlocal_msoa_annual_office_indicators_2019_2025.csv` | Annual office stock, unit-size, floorspace, value and occupation indicators within the five office submarkets |
| `openlocal_office_adjustment_panel_2019_2025.csv` | Office adjustment components and the derived fragmentation and persistent-large-office measures |

The tables deliberately exclude property addresses, individual records, tenant information, Green Street data and Green Street-derived fields. They are complete for their stated study scope, but they are not the full property-level database. For property-level replication, postcode reconciliation or construction of alternative indicators, obtain the full OpenLocal release from the [official OpenLocal service](https://openlocal.uk/) and follow [the external-input instructions](../../external/README.md).
