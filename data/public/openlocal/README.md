# OpenLocal annual retail indicators

`openlocal_msoa_annual_retail_indicators_2019_2025.csv` is a public, OpenLocal-only MSOA-year table prepared from the openly licensed commercial-property source. It includes annual retail records and unit counts, total and median rateable value, total and median floor area, the number of vacant records and the occupation-based vacancy proxy.

The table deliberately excludes property addresses, individual records, tenant information, Green Street data, Green Street-derived fields and origin-destination exposure measures. It is suitable for inspecting the public administrative-property component of the residential analysis. For property-level replication, obtain the full OpenLocal source from its official service.
