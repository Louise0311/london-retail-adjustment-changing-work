# Reproducible public derivatives

These files are generated by `notebooks/01_Data_Audit_Spatial_Framework.ipynb` from the public TfL annualised entry/exit tables and public Underground station-point geometry.

They contain the station-level Monday and Friday ratios, annual deficits, cumulative 2023-2025 commuter-demand shock score, rank, Top-100 flag and station geometry. They do not contain Green Street data or Green Street-derived information.

The CSV geometry field is stored as WKT for compatibility with downstream notebooks. Equivalent GeoJSON files are included for direct GIS inspection.

The folder also contains the public-data spatial chain used in Objective 1:

- `objective1_selected_workplace_stations.csv`: the 48 Top-100 stations retained by the five-submarket and 500 m rule;
- `objective1_selected_workplace_msoa_targets.csv`: the 27 workplace MSOAs containing those stations and their activity-weighted shock scores;
- `objective1_residential_msoa_exposure.csv`: the 975 residential MSOA exposure values constructed from the complete ONS commuting matrix;
- `objective1_workplace_openlocal_msoa_year.csv` and `objective1_workplace_openlocal_change_panel.csv`: public OpenLocal outcomes joined to the workplace shock frame.

All files in this folder are generated from public TfL, ONS, boundary and OpenLocal inputs. They contain no Green Street field or derivative.
